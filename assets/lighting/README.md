Loaded as a linear floating-point equirectangular HDR texture, with mipmaps for approximate rough reflections. This is image-based lighting, combined with two analytic studio lights; it is not a realtime reflection of the pieces or ray tracing. The shader uses GGX direct specular, Schlick Fresnel, material-dependent roughness, a clear-coat approximation and ACES-style tone mapping. Environment mipmaps are a lightweight approximation, not a GGX-prefiltered cubemap.
